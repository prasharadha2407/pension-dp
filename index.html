    How it works

    What is a pension cut?

    If the fund holds too little money to keep paying everyone, the rules make it lower the pension for all members. This tool counts how often that happens.

    What is coverage (CCR)?

    The fund's money divided by what it owes in future pensions. The rules want it between 100% and 125%. Below 100% pensions are cut. Above 125% they are raised.

    What do the shaded bands mean?

    Dark band: the middle half of outcomes. Light band: 9 in 10 outcomes. Solid line: typical case. Dashed orange line: average.

    Is this financial advice?

    No. It is a study model based on a research paper. Results depend on model assumptions and the detail level chosen.
